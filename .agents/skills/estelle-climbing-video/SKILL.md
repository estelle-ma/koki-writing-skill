# Estelle Climbing Video

## Non-negotiable integrity

Footage comes from Estelle's actual recordings and any explicitly licensed inserts. Do not combine different attempts into a fake continuous send/top, change route grade or conditions, or invent achievements, injuries, measurements or training claims. Ask for manual review when a cut might obscure whether a hold, start or finish is valid. Protect hands, feet and hold locations when reframing.

## Select the edit type

1. **Outdoor documentary:** show rock/environment, route context, natural sound and an honestly paced climbing attempt. National Geographic-like restraint; quiet captions for location/date/route when supplied.
2. **Indoor bouldering performance:** let viewers read start, sequence, crux, top or fall. Slow down or replay decisive moves only with clearly signaled replays; avoid distracting transitions.
3. **Technique / sport science:** show annotated holds, body position, weight shift and attempt comparison without false biomechanical certainty. Specify observation versus inferred mechanism, and cite published claims.

## Workflow

- Ingest clips, inspect duration, orientation, audio, and establish an attempt log with source clip + in/out times.
- Produce an **edit decision list** before cutting. The author approves which attempts to include and the intended story.
- Use local FFmpeg for trimming, sync, reframing, audio and verified export. Use Remotion only when adding text, routes, vectors or explainers. Koki/ChatCut may handle narrated explanation and captions; do not assume it can reliably detect climbing sends.
- Avoid dramatic speed ramps across critical movements unless clearly identified; preserve at least one readable sequence of each featured key move. Replays and multi-angle composites should be labeled.
- For 9:16 outputs, inspect handholds, footholds, climber and wall context in the full movement range. If crop hides relevant information, use reframing, picture-in-picture or letterboxing rather than cropping the move out.
- Export a review cut; check factual accuracy, sound levels, legible typography and captions before a final deliverable.

Never upload personal footage to Sparki/EachLabs or trigger a potentially paid action without explicit approval. Never publish automatically. For proposed cuts, supply a timestamped shot list and note any unresolved ambiguity.
